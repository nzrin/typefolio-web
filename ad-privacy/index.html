Ad Privacy Choices

A clear view of advertising in Type Folio and the controls available to you.

Last updated 3 November 2026

✦

Rewarded ads are currently Coming Soon.The app’s Watch Ad button is not active. This website does not display advertising or load an advertising SDK.

1. Current status

Type Folio includes a planned rewarded-ad option in the coin store. In the current version, it is labeled “Watch Ad · Coming Soon” and cannot be used to view ads or earn coins.

There is no advertising consent control on this website because it does not run an advertising service.

2. If ads become available

If a future release enables rewarded ads, this page and the Privacy Policy will explain the provider, information processed, and applicable choices. Any consent or privacy options required for that release will be presented through the app.

Availability, reward amounts, and viewing limits will be shown in the app. A planned feature does not guarantee a launch date.

3. Device-level choices

Your device provides privacy settings for app permissions and tracking. You can review these in iOS or Android Settings. Device settings and an app’s own consent settings may control different things.

Changing a device setting does not delete your Type Folio account or saved progress. For deletion, use the Account Deletion instructions.

4. Questions about advertising

If you see an ad or privacy behavior that does not match this description, contact support@typefolioapp.nazrin.codes with your app version and platform. Our Privacy Policy explains the rest of the app’s data handling.
