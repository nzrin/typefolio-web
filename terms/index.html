2. Guest play & accounts

You can begin as a guest. Guest progress is held on your device and may be lost if you remove the app, clear its data, or lose the device. Sign in to access account-based progress and synchronization.

Keep your sign-in details secure and use an account you are entitled to access. Do not impersonate another player. Signing out, changing accounts, or signing in on another device may change which progress and coin balance you see. Contact us if something looks wrong.

3. Progress & leaderboards

Gameplay can award points, stars, and achievements. Your public player name, avatar, score, and rank may appear on the global leaderboard. Rankings change as scores are recorded and other players progress.

Do not alter game data, automate play to gain an unfair advantage, falsify purchase records, or exploit bugs to obtain coins or scores. We may correct invalid progress or restrict misuse of online features. Contact us if you believe a correction is mistaken.

Coins can be used for missions, custom spaces, and available boosts. The app shows the cost before you spend. Coins and game rewards cannot be transferred to other players or exchanged for money.

Coin packages, where available, are consumable in-app purchases. You must sign in before buying. Review the package and local store price before approving payment. Your app store processes the payment, while Type Folio verifies the transaction and credits the associated account.

Restoring purchases does not refill coins already spent or duplicate a previously credited transaction. Refund eligibility and processing follow your store’s rules and applicable law. See Purchases & Refunds for help.
