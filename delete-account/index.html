Delete Your Account

Leave your orbit on your terms. Here is how to delete a Type Folio account and what happens to its information.

Last updated 3 November 2026

✦

Deletion is permanent.Your profile, saved game progress, custom spaces, and account coin balance will no longer be available. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete an online account.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open Type Folio and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and choose Delete Account.
  4. Complete the confirmation steps. Apple sign-in may ask you to verify your identity again.

If the app asks you to remove Type Folio from Sign in with Apple after deletion, follow the instructions in your Apple Account settings.

2. Cannot access the app?

Email support@typefolioapp.nazrin.codes with the subject “Type Folio account deletion.” Include the email linked to the account and, if known, your username and sign-in method.

For Sign in with Apple, your account may use an Apple private relay address. Tell us if that applies. We will verify ownership before acting on a deletion request. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or full payment information.

3. What is removed

Deletion removes the authentication account and its linked player profile, cloud progress, custom spaces, and leaderboard records. Access to the account’s coin balance ends. Other devices may still show cached information until they next validate the account or reconnect.

Guest progress and operating-system backups are separate from the online account. Remove the app’s local data if you also want to clear data left on a device.

4. What can remain

Verified purchase records are retained to prevent repeat redemption and support transaction integrity. The account link is removed, but product, transaction, and verification information may remain. Provider backups, operational logs, or records required for legal or security purposes may also persist under the relevant retention requirements.

See the Privacy Policy for more detail. You can ask support about retained information associated with your request.

5. Before you confirm

Resolve any missing purchase first and make sure you are deleting the intended account. Deletion cannot be undone and does not automatically initiate a store refund. Read Purchases & Refunds if you have a payment question.
