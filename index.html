Where can I get Type Folio?+

Type Folio is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play, for phones and tablets. Download links will appear here when it launches.

Can I play without an account?+

Yes. Start exploring as a guest. Sign in to save your progress across devices and take your place on the global leaderboard. An account is required to purchase coins.

What are coins used for?+

Coins let you start missions, create custom spaces, and use boosts such as Freeze and First Miss Shield. The app shows the coin cost before you spend.

Can I choose my own words?+

Yes. In Explore, create a custom space, choose your difficulty, and add your own words. The app guides you through the word requirements for each difficulty.
